EVENTORA — Admin & Staff Rulebook

  1. Participant information and payment evidence are confidential. Share only for an authorised event task.
  2. All five Staff accounts may view registration records and submitted payment evidence once the payment module is enabled.
  3. Only the two designated Payment Verifiers and the Main Admin may approve or reject payments.
  4. General Staff may view records but cannot make payment decisions or change participant approval status.
  5. The Main Admin alone controls pricing, Early Bird settings, staff roles and participant approval or rejection.
  6. Never share passwords. Each person must use their own account.
  7. Important actions must remain traceable in the audit history.
  8. The Main Admin may correct participant decisions and disable or restore staff access.
