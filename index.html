About

About EVENTORA

EVENTORA is a prototype for a calm, reliable way to discover, prepare for and manage events. It keeps the important information visible: what happens, when, where, under what rules, and by when to register.

01

Discover

Browse by category, search by keyword and sort by date or deadline.

02

Prepare

Read rules, eligibility and deadlines before you commit.

03

Participate

Register, receive confirmation and, in future versions, carry a verified digital ID.

How verification would work

  1. Register
    Provide your name, email and institution.
  2. Confirm email
    Use a one-time link (planned for a future version).
  3. Institution check
    An organizer reviews the request. This is never automatic.
  4. Issue ID
    A unique code and optional QR check-in are generated.

In this prototype none of these steps run. The card above is a static design sample.
